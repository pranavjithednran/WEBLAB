Master Of Computer Application

The Master of Computer Applications (MCA) program at Ilahia College of Engineering and Technology (ICET) in Muvattupuzha is a two-year, full-time postgraduate course divided into four semesters. Established in 2008, the department has an intake capacity of 60 students and focuses on blending theoretical foundations with practical programming skills to prepare students for dynamic IT careers. Eligibility requires a recognized bachelor's degree (such as a BCA or related field) with a minimum aggregate of 50% marks, alongside mathematics at the 10+2 or graduation level, and a valid score in the relevant state entrance examination. In addition to the standard curriculum, the department enhances real-world learning through technical seminars, workshops, industrial visits, and a final semester entirely dedicated to project
