Ilahia College of Engineering and Technology (ICET), established in 2002, is an AICTE-approved institution affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU). Located in Mulavoor, it offers comprehensive professional education including B.Tech, M.Tech, MBA, MCA, and Ph.D. programs across multiple specializations. The campus features modern academic infrastructure, practical research facilities, and a dedicated training and placement cell to support student careers. For inquiries, the campus can be reached at 0485 2549145 or via their official website at icet.ac.in.
